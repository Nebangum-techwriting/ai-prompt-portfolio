Cinematic African Love & Betrayal

Project Overview

This project is a 10-scene cinematic African visual narrative exploring love, family, betrayal, heartbreak, healing, and new beginnings.

The project demonstrates how AI prompt engineering can be used to translate a written story into a sequence of visually connected scenes.

Creative Objective

The goal was to create a cinematic story with:

- Consistent character identity
- Consistent wardrobe
- Emotional progression
- Clear scene-to-scene continuity
- African cultural representation
- Cinematic composition
- Story-driven visual direction

Story Structure

The narrative follows a relationship from love and family life through betrayal and heartbreak, eventually leading to healing and a new beginning.

Prompt Engineering Approach

Character Continuity

Core physical characteristics, wardrobe details, and visual identity were maintained across the story to help preserve continuity.

Emotional Progression

Each prompt was designed around the emotional purpose of the scene rather than simply describing what should appear in the image.

Cinematic Direction

Prompts included:

- Camera framing
- Lighting
- Depth of field
- Composition
- Facial expression
- Body language
- Environment
- Emotional atmosphere

Scene Continuity

The prompts were structured sequentially so that each image contributes to the larger narrative.
