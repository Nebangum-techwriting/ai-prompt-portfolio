NAYA SKIN — Glow Naturally

Project Overview

NAYA SKIN — Glow Naturally is a fictional premium African skincare campaign created as an AI visual content and prompt-engineering project.

The campaign was designed to demonstrate how structured AI prompts can be used to create cohesive commercial visuals for a beauty brand.

Campaign Objective

The objective was to develop a premium visual identity for an African skincare brand and create multiple advertising visuals while maintaining consistency in:

- Brand identity
- Product presentation
- Lighting
- Composition
- Styling
- African representation
- Commercial photography aesthetics

Visual Campaign

The campaign consists of six primary visuals:

1. Hero Advertisement
2. Luxury Product Shot
3. Lifestyle Advertisement
4. Social Media Promotional Visual
5. Product & Ingredients Visual
6. Campaign Poster

Prompt Engineering Approach

The prompts were developed to communicate the intended commercial direction clearly to the AI image-generation system.

Each prompt specified relevant elements such as:

- Product placement
- Model styling
- Skin appearance
- Lighting
- Background
- Camera composition
- Color atmosphere
- Props
- Advertising space
- Premium beauty aesthetic

Key Prompt Engineering Techniques

Brand Consistency

The prompts maintained a consistent premium African beauty identity across the campaign.

Product Placement

Product positioning was carefully specified to ensure that the skincare product remained visually important within each composition.

Commercial Photography Direction

Prompts included lighting, composition, styling, realism, and advertising language to guide the AI toward professional commercial imagery.

Audience Representation

The campaign incorporated African representation as an important part of the visual identity.

Controlled Variation

Different campaign formats were created while maintaining a cohesive overall visual language.
